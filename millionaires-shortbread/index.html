Millionaires' Shortbread

Crunchy shortbread base, soft condensed-milk caramel and a layer of chocolate on top. Makes about 24 squares.

Method

  1. Heat the oven and prepare the tin

    Heat the oven to 180°C conventional / 160°C fan / Gas 4. Lightly grease a 33 × 23 cm swiss roll tin.

  2. Rub in the butter

    Stir the flour and caster sugar together in a bowl. Rub in the softened butter with your fingertips until the mix looks like fine breadcrumbs.

  3. Bake the base

    Squeeze and knead the crumbs into a dough, then press it evenly over the base of the tin. Prick all over lightly with a fork. Bake for about 20 minutes, until firm to the touch and very pale golden. Leave to cool in the tin.

  4. Make the caramel

    Put the butter, muscovado sugar and condensed milk in a pan and warm gently until the sugar has dissolved. Bring to the boil, stirring constantly, then turn the heat down and simmer very gently, still stirring, for about 5 minutes until slightly thickened. Do not stop stirring, as it catches and burns on the bottom of the pan very quickly. Pour over the shortbread and leave to cool.

  5. Add the chocolate and cut

    Break the chocolate into pieces and melt gently in a bowl over a pan of hot water, stirring now and then. Pour over the cold caramel and leave to set. Cut into squares or bars.

Notes

  • Marbled top. Melt a little over 50 g each of plain, milk and white chocolate in three separate bowls. Spoon them over the set caramel in turn, then drag a skewer through the edges where they meet to swirl them together, and leave to set.
