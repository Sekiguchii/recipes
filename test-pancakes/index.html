Simple Pancakes

Fluffy everyday pancakes. Test page for the recipe publishing workflow.

Serves 4Prep 10 minCook 15 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 2 eggs
  • 300 ml milk
  • 2 tbsp melted butter, plus extra for the pan

Method

  1. Whisk the flour, baking powder, sugar and salt in a large bowl.
  2. In a jug, whisk the eggs, milk and melted butter.
  3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry and stir until just combined; a few lumps are fine.
  4. Heat a lightly buttered pan over medium heat and cook ladlefuls of batter until bubbles form, then flip and cook 1 minute more.
  5. Serve warm with your favourite toppings.
